# Senior Distributed Systems Engineer

**Company:** Acme Corp
**Location:** Remote (US)
**Base Salary:** $150,000 - $190,000 + Equity

## About Acme Corp
We are building the next generation of resilient infrastructure for global logistics. We value high agency, engineering excellence, and a deep understanding of distributed systems.

## The Role
As a Senior Distributed Systems Engineer, you will lead the architecture and implementation of our core message-broker system that handles millions of events per minute.

## What You'll Do
* Architect, build, and maintain highly available distributed systems in Go.
* Design and implement robust API boundaries and event-driven architectures.
* Mentor mid-level engineers and drive technical design discussions.
* Optimize systems for latency, throughput, and reliability.

## Qualifications
* 5+ years of software engineering experience.
* Strong proficiency in Go, Rust, or C++.
* Deep understanding of distributed systems concepts (consensus, replication, partitioning).
* Experience with Kafka, Redis, or similar technologies.
* Proven track record of operating services at scale.

## Nice to Have
* Experience with Kubernetes and cloud-native ecosystems.
* Open-source contributions to distributed data stores.
